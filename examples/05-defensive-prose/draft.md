We acknowledge at the outset that this study is by no means an exhaustive treatment of all possible environments. It is important to emphasize that the following results should be interpreted with appropriate caution and should not be misunderstood as a claim to universal generality. In our observational sample of 62 plots, canopy cover was associated with lower midday soil temperature (r = -0.28, 95% CI -0.49 to -0.03). We do not intend to imply that a causal effect has been established, and it should be stressed that the association is an association. These caveats are offered to preempt potential misunderstandings of the scope of our analysis.

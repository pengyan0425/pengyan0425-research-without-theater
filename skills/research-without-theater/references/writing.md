# Research prose: preserve the argument and the evidence

## Build around a claim

Identify the question, the finding that answers it, and the evidence supporting that finding. Draft paragraphs around their role in that argument. Let the contribution set the order; a list of completed analyses rarely supplies the paper's logic.

For local edits, identify the exact requested passage and retain the surrounding content. Repair a dependency elsewhere only when it is necessary and authorized. Follow a user's discussion-before-editing instruction when present.

## Judge the function of each sentence

Keep sentences that establish the problem, report a result, connect evidence to an explanation, develop the contribution, or specify an interpretation-changing limit. Remove sentences whose only purpose is to anticipate an imagined objection, reassure the reader about diligence, or repeat an already clear qualification.

Prefer a positive description of what the study estimates and supports. Use a qualification once, where it changes the reading. Keep a negative formulation when it is needed for accuracy or directly answers the user's question.

Example with invented values:

> We acknowledge that our study is by no means exhaustive. It is important to emphasize that these results should be interpreted cautiously. In our observational sample of 48 catchments, vegetation cover was associated with lower peak runoff (r = -0.31, 95% CI -0.54 to -0.03). We do not intend to imply that causation has been conclusively established.

Becomes:

> Across 48 observed catchments, vegetation cover was associated with lower peak runoff (r = -0.31, 95% CI -0.54 to -0.03).

The observational design and association language preserve the evidence boundary. If the user's argument turns on causal identification, explain the actual identification limit where it belongs.

## Keep limits that matter

An interval spanning zero, an influential exclusion, an adverse subgroup result, a design constraint, or a failed replication can change the finding. Preserve it with the relevant claim. Move procedural detail to Methods or supplementary material when the main interpretation remains visible and accurate.

For example, if a pooled estimate is positive but a prespecified spatial holdout estimate is near zero, write both into the central result. Removing the holdout finding to create a cleaner story changes the evidence.

## Compression is an evidence-preserving edit

Check the revised passage against the source for direction, numbers, units, sample, uncertainty, and strength of inference. Preserve exploratory labels. Avoid turning association into an intervention effect, an uncertain estimate into evidence of no effect, or a proposed analysis into a completed one.

Use the supplied evidence. Mark an unavailable fact precisely, such as `[sample size needed]`, only where needed in a draft. In final-facing prose, resolve that fact from a source or tell the user what is still missing. Complete independent sentences without inventing missing details.
